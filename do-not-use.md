# Do not use

Lives at the **root of `flaplab-dailies`**. Grok Bot must read this every run. Humans append; the bot does not edit this file.

This is a **quality + content** kill-list, not a copyright list. Franchise sprites, wiki rips, and official-looking renders are allowed.

## Content (hard)

Skip the concept if the joke or the files are:

- Nudity / sexual content / porn
- Drugs as the subject or punchline (product, use, paraphernalia)
- Gore (guts, corpses, torture, injury-as-the-joke)

Horror icons, weapons, explosions, dark humor, politics, and IP skins are fine.

## Asset sources (quality)

Do not download from hosts that repeatedly serve:

- Fake “transparent” PNGs (white/gray box, checkerboard baked into pixels)
- Heavy watermarks / SEO-farm stamps across the sprite
- Broken or malware “png download” pages that are not an image

Add a host here the first time it burns a morning (cron skip / opaque crop).
