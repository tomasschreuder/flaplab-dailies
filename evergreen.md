# Evergreen fallbacks

Lives at the **root of `flaplab-dailies`**. Use these when today’s scout has no shippable art. Still search for **transparent** bird + pillar; these are concepts, not bundled files.

Prefer a live joke when assets exist. These keep the **assigned slot** honest instead of shipping a hollow trend.

## cat

- Orange loaf / sitting tabby / wet cat. Pillars: yarn, rubber duck, shampoo bottle. Sky: bathroom or living room.
- Physics: readable flap, default-ish speed.

## fun

- Rocket or astronaut, planet/flag pillars, starfield or moon. **Low gravity.**
- Jet or helicopter, bomb or missile GIF pillars (tight column), terrain. **`fly` + higher speed.**

## recognisable

Pick a face people clock in 0.3s and matching world pillars:

- MLP pony + crystal/castle
- Portal core + Aperture panel
- Garfield + lasagna stack
- House + cane / Vicodin-bottle is **drugs — skip that object**; use hospital / cane / whiteboard
- COD soldier + zombie
- Terraria/Factorio character + biome machine / corrupt pillar
- KFC colonel + bucket
- Breaking Bad: face + RV or desert (not powder)
- Sopranos: Tony + gabagool / pork store

## meme

If the viral thing is a video you cannot still, drop to a visual meme format: countryball reaction, Wojak vs Chad as bird vs pillar, exploding-brain as stacked pillars.

## country

- Country **in the news this week** as countryball, landmark or flag as pillars, flag as sky.
- France / baguette / Eiffel if news is quiet.

## political / story

- Leader face / opposition face / flag sky.
- One-sentence fable: bird wants X, pillars are the thing that stops X. No gore, no drugs, no nudity.
